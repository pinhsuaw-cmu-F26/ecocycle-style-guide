EcoCycle visual style guide
Purpose: help people extend electronics’ useful life through accessible assessment, repair guidance, local services, and rewards.
Brand & visual foundations
E/C monogram: the plug and wire suggest E; the green circular arrow forms C. Together they connect electronics with repair, reuse, and a longer device life; user-supplied approved darker-orange logo artwork with charcoal lettering. Keep the full logo visible with clear space. Forest #245342 anchors identity; orange #AF421D identifies actions; warm white #FFF7EE provides the canvas; charcoal #292D29 is the default for headings, navigation, and body text.
Typography & layout
Headings: Montserrat SemiBold (600). Body, navigation, buttons, and forms: Open Sans / Arial / sans-serif. Body Regular (400); labels and buttons SemiBold (600). Titles 32px; section headings 24px; body 16px; labels 14px; secondary captions 12px. Use a 4px spacing scale, 24px card padding, 12px card radius, and controls at least 44px high. Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
Interaction principles
Familiar language, minimal repeated input, visual instructions with concise explanations, immediate feedback, error prevention, and focused task flows. Safety warnings remain visible.
Core UI elements
Logo, colors, typography, spacing, buttons, Lucide outline icons, forms, validation, feedback, navigation, context menus, dialogs, tables, and status labels. Styles and applicable interaction states are included in the CSS library.

Cards — repair options.
Progressive disclosure — repair guidance.
Search filters — local services.
Dashboard — reward tracking.
